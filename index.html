Troubleshooting

Common Washer Issues

  • Not starting: Check power supply and door latch.
  • Not draining: Check the drain hose for kinks or clogs.
  • Loud noise: Possible foreign object or unbalanced load — pause and redistribute items.

If these steps don't help, request service and we'll diagnose it quickly.

Common Dryer Issues

  • Not heating: Confirm correct hookup (gas vs electric) and circuit breaker.
  • Not tumbling: Check drive belt or motor (we can dispatch repair).
  • Loud vibration: Ensure dryer is level and lint trap is clean.

Still having trouble? We’ll swap or repair the unit under your plan.

Before You Call

  1. Try a power cycle: unplug for 60 seconds then reconnect.
  2. Check hoses and vents for obvious obstructions.
  3. Note any error codes and the exact behavior — helps our techs diagnose faster.
